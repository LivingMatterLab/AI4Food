BBQ BURGERS 
3 lbs. hamburger
2 med. onions, minced
2 cans tomato soup
2 tsp. prepared mustard
Salt and pepper to taste
1 tbsp. chili powder
1/2 tsp. Tabasco sauce

Brown hamburger and onions and drain. Add other ingredients and heat thoroughly. Makes about 12 burgers.
