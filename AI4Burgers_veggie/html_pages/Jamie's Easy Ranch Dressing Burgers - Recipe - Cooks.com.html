JAMIE'S EASY RANCH DRESSING
BURGERS		 
1/2 env. HIDDEN VALLEY® dry ranch dressing mix
1 lb. ground beef

Mix dressing and ground beef. Form into patties and cook as desired. Grilled is best to me.

Serve on toasted buns with cheese.
