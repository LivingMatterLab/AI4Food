JACK O'LANTERN BURGERS 
2 lb. ground beef
1 tbsp. Worcestershire sauce
1 1/2 tsp. salt
pepper, to taste
6 slices American cheese

In a large bowl, combine beef, Worcestershire sauce, and salt and pepper. Form into 6 equal-sized patties.

Heat a large nonstick skillet over medium heat. Cook burgers, turning once, about 4 to 5 minutes each side or to desired doneness.

Meanwhile, using a sharp knife, cut out Jack O'Lantern faces in slices of cheese. Set on wax paper or parchment paper.

When burgers are cooked, transfer to a serving platter and immediately place carved cheese slices atop burgers.

Serve on buns if desired, or open-faced on just the bun bottom if you don't wish to smother the Jack O'Lantern while enjoying your burger. :-)
